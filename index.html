Our story

We started with one simple idea: queer people deserve space to be fully seen.

I’m Alex, a non-binary lesbian and the founder of DMV Queers. After experiencing discrimination and isolation in a heteronormative society, I began to understand how much could change when I surrounded myself with queer people.

After leaving college, I wanted more queer connection but did not know where to begin. I started a Snapchat group with queer friends across the DMV, and our first picnic brought four of us together. We had a great time, and I realized this could become something bigger.

I founded DMV Queers in September 2021, when I was 21, to create a community for 18+ queer people living in the DMV. I wanted to build a safe space where LGBTQ+ people could feel welcomed, respected, and loved for being their authentic selves.

I shared the story behind DMV Queers on Instagram in October 2021. Today, the organization is rooted in that same belief: queer people deserve connection, safety, joy, and community without having to hide who they are.
