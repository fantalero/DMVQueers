About us

We built this organization for queer people who needed a place to belong.

Our story

I’m Alex, a non-binary lesbian and the founder of DMV Queers. Like many queer people, I have experienced discrimination because of my sexual orientation and gender identity. Living in a heteronormative society, I learned that in spaces that did not accommodate me, denial, silence, and a depoliticized version of myself could feel like the price of acceptance.

That began to change when I found myself surrounded by queer people. After leaving college, I wanted to build more queer connection in my life, but I did not know where to begin. I started a Snapchat group for my queer friends across the DMV, and we planned a picnic. Only four of us were able to attend, but we had an amazing time. That small gathering showed me how much possibility could grow from simply bringing queer people together.

Why it was created

I founded DMV Queers in September 2021, when I was 21, with a clear purpose: to create a community for 18+ queer people living in the DMV. I wanted to build a safe space where LGBTQ+ people could feel welcomed, respected, and loved for who they are, without having to make themselves smaller to belong.

I shared the story behind DMV Queers on Instagram in October 2021. The organization continues to grow from that first picnic and that original vision: queer people deserve connection, safety, joy, and a place where they can show up as their full, authentic selves.

Mission

What we stand for

Belonging

We create spaces where queer people can be fully themselves without fear, shame, or exclusion.

Mutual aid

We support neighbors through care, resource sharing, and collective problem-solving.

Visibility

We uplift queer stories, leadership, and experiences so our communities remain seen and valued.

Community values

How we show up

Accessibility

We center access, inclusion, and practical support so more people can participate.

Care

We create events and programs that respond to real needs and make our community feel safer.

Action

We move from care into organizing, advocacy, and support because we believe change requires both.
